## 🇨🇳 中文

面向广告宣传语、营销海报文案、社媒推广文案和直播话术的文字合规审查技能。它对素材逐条拆解，覆盖虚假宣传、夸大宣传、绝对化用语、比较广告、引证数据真实准确性、价格违规、有奖营销、知识产权、特殊监管行业（金融、医疗、三品一械、教育等）违规宣传、数据对外披露、不当承诺与表达等维度，把违规点分类分级、逐条分析判断，并直接给出修改建议。整个流程分五步：逐条拆解文案、检索适用规则并标注来源、分类检查、对照产品检验，最后以飞书文档交付完整审查报告。它只审文字，不撰写营销文案、不证实宣传内容是否真实、也不审查素材设计或图像。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 粘一段广告文案 → 逐条拆出隐藏的违规点并分类分级
- 命中绝对化用语或比较广告 → 直接给出可替换的改法
- 引证数据、价格促销、有奖规则 → 单独核查并指出需补证据之处
- 金融/医疗/三品一械/教育等特殊行业 → 走更严的监管口径检查
- 每条判断标注适用规则来源 → 便于内部合规复核与留档
- 输出飞书文档审查报告 → 违规点归类 + 逐条分析 + 修改建议

## 🇬🇧 English

A text-only compliance review skill for ad slogans, poster copy, social posts and livestream scripts. It breaks material down line by line and scans it across false advertising, exaggeration, absolute claims, comparative advertising, citation accuracy, pricing violations, prize promotions, intellectual property, regulated industries such as finance, medical, health products and education, public disclosure of data, and improper promises. Findings are classified, analyzed one by one, and returned with concrete rewrite suggestions. The workflow runs five steps: deconstruct the copy, retrieve the applicable rules with source labels, classify and check, verify against the actual product, and deliver a full review report as a Feishu doc. It reviews text only: it does not write marketing copy, does not verify whether claims are true, and does not review visual design.

### ✨ What it does

- Paste ad copy → get every violation pulled out, classified and graded
- Absolute claims or comparative ads flagged → concrete replacement wording offered
- Citation accuracy, pricing promotions and prize rules checked → gaps flagged as needing evidence
- Finance, medical, health products and education → screened against stricter regulated-industry rules
- Every finding tagged with the rule it cites → easy internal compliance sign-off
- Delivers a Feishu review doc → grouped violations, line-by-line analysis, rewrite suggestions
